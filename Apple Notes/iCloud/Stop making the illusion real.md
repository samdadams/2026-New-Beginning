Total escape from past no

For a long time i have been seeing everyone as having two faces. I have not always been consistent in remembering which face is really true. But just before Natalies passing we went to see her and despite what was said during the visit as we left the body of Natalie was in pain and needing rest. All i could see was the true shining face of Natalie. She was not in pain she was not suffering. She was smiling, happy and content and that is how I continually see her now. 
When people seem to pass i do not have the same experience as most. I do not feel sad. I actually feel a peace and happiness with it, not because they go to some magical place in the sky or Heaven. But because i just have this knowing they never left.
