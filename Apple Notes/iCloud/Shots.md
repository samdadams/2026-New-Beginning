On new years day I unintentionally boarded the train back to Colchester without a valid ticket, which I have never done before. I regularly travel to and through London and so use the train a lot to get to work and travel to different airports etc.  I had bought a month return ticket that I had from before (as I don’t always return through London) and I am a carer so I travel about to clients addresses.

Originally I was only supposed to work until New Years eve(which my ticket was valid for) but I was asked to do the new years night shift, which meant that unknowingly my ticket expired, I will include the picture if I can.  I was so exhausted from doing the nightshift I wasn’t thinking clearly and didn’t check to see if my ticket was stick valid. 

I just want to say again that I have never boarded a train without a valid ticket ever and this was just a silly mistake and the hundreds of tickets I have bought over the last few years will show this.  I don’t earn a lot and this is just another burden to add to things.  

I have had a railcard and so many ticket purchases going back a few year that I will screenshot to prove I would never intentionally board a train without a valid ticket and will make sure this never happens again.

Kind Regards Samuel Adams
