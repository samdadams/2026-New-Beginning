Struggles following Guidance 

Deciding where to go and what to do and listen to the inner voice consistently

I have struggled with this for the past 20 year despite the consistent practice. 

Something that has helped me so much recently is to keep allowing the outcome and all my attachments to any outcome to be dropped in the moment. 

The sneaky old outcomes come back in and I forget my real purpose to forgot what I think I need

When I forget what I think the answer often comes that the thing I wanted was just a substitute for what I needed.

The hardest thing to deal with is the emotional shocks send me back into past habits or past reactions and those are the moments or necessary times just for a moment to stop and stay still or go within release everything and say I don’t know what I need in this moment. 

Surrender is so difficult when I feel attached to the outcome and secretly the outcome may be hidden from my vision, until I truly let go of what I think I need and it often comes with a moment of relief and the realisation I don’t actually need that thing and don’t even want it because it takes me away from what I really want which is peace. 

I often receive the correct guidance or what I should do and I don’t listen to the stimulus. Like smoking, my body tells me that it doesn’t like it or my peace is lost because I am in a relationship that doesn’t serve me. A relationship being a situation which can be momentary or last a long time. 

Getting caught in webs of thinking my happiness is outside me or thinking if a scene had gone differently
