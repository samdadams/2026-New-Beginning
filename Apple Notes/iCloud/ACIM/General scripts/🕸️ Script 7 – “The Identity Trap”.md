**🕸️ 7.** ***“The Identity Trap”***
Letting go of roles—especially spiritual ones—returns you to your formless, holy Self. It frees you from performance and invites true peace, beyond image.

**Theme**: Letting go of spiritual roles and false self-concepts **Length**: ~20 minutes **ACIM Quotes**:
“The ego is nothing more than a part of your belief about yourself.” (T-4.VI.1:6) “The self you made is not the Son of God.” (T-4.II.3:8) “I am not a body. I am free. For I am still as God created me.” (W-201)

**🌑 [Opening – 0:00–2:00]**
Welcome.
Let this be a soft unraveling. A moment of deep honesty. Where we lay down not just stress… …but identity.
Let your breath come slowly. And your exhale signal this release:
“I don’t need to be anyone right now. I don’t need to perform anything here.”

**🎭 [Phase 1 – The Roles We Wear – 2:00–7:00]**
Bring to mind a role you’ve been playing recently. Maybe as a helper. A guide. A teacher. A healer. A good parent. A spiritual one.
What does this role require of you? How do you feel when you're not performing it well?
Pause. Feel what it takes to maintain this identity.
Now ask:
**“Who am I trying to be? And who would I be if I let that go?”**
Let the body respond. Not with effort. But with honesty.
You may feel sadness. Relief. Fear. Lightness.
Whatever arises—welcome it.
Say:
“I am not the image I protect. I am not the role I play.”

**🪞 [Phase 2 – Undoing the Trap – 7:00–13:00]**
A Course in Miracles tells us:
“The self you made is not the Son of God.” (T-4.II.3:8)
You were not created as a spiritual success story. A brand. An archetype.
Those are masks. Helpful for learning, maybe— but not the truth.
Let us step back from the mask now.
See yourself before you were spiritual. Before you were useful. Before you were understood.
And even now—beneath all the roles— what remains?
Breathe there.
Say silently:
“I am not a body. I am free. For I am still as God created me.” (W-201)
No performance. No polishing. Only presence.

**🌬️ [Phase 3 – Trusting the Unformed Self – 13:00–17:00]**
It takes courage to not know who you are. To stop clinging to spiritual identity as a form of safety.
But this is where grace enters.
The ego is terrified of undefinition. But truth is found in the formless.
Ask now:
**“If I don’t know who I am… can I rest in that?”**
Say silently:
“I no longer need to prove I am good. I no longer need to be seen as awakened. I am not a spiritual identity. I am the light behind all masks.”
Feel the loosening. Feel the permission.

**🌌 [Closing – 17:00–20:00]**
Let the breath return again.
Feel the open space inside you. Not a void. But a vastness.
A Course in Miracles says:
“The ego is nothing more than a part of your belief about yourself.” (T-4.VI.1:6)
You are not the belief. You are the awareness.
And that awareness needs no title, no proof, no applause.
Say gently:
“I rest in the truth of what I am. Beyond roles. Beyond image. Beyond form.”
Let that awareness lead your next conversation. Your next post. Your next silence.
Thank you for being real.
You are the light of God, not the story of awakening.
