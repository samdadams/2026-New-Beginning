**🪞 4.** ***“What I Judge, I Bind”***
Judgment fixes both you and others in roles that block healing. Practicing nonjudgment allows freedom, movement, and a way out of mental suffering.

**Theme**: How judgment traps both self and others in illusion **Length**: ~20 minutes **ACIM Quotes**:
“You see what you expect, and you expect what you invite.” (T-12.VII.5:1) “What you believe you are determines your gifts, and if God created you perfect, you are offering peace to everyone.” (T-14.V.9:1) “Do not see error. Look past it.” (T-9.IV.1:2–3)

**🌬️ [Opening Grounding – 0:00–2:00]**
Welcome. This is a space to release the weight of judgment— judgment of others, of yourself, of the world.
Let your breath guide you inward. Feel the body soften. Feel the thoughts begin to slow. There is no need to control anything here.
We will not fight judgment today. We will watch it. And let it dissolve in light.

**🔍 [Phase 1 – Witnessing Judgment – 2:00–7:00]**
Bring to mind someone you’ve recently judged. Maybe for how they spoke to you… for how they behaved… or simply for not meeting your expectations.
See them clearly in your mind now. Let the story play, just for a moment. The thought: “They should be different.” “They’re wrong.” “They’re the problem.”
Now pause.
Feel how this judgment feels in your body. A contraction. A separation. A closing.
Say silently: **“This is what judgment does. It cuts me off from peace.”**
And now, instead of following the thought— Step back.
Ask:
“What am I seeing? And who is the one who sees?”
You are not the thought. You are the one noticing the thought. And that changes everything.

**🔗 [Phase 2 – Judgment Binds the Self – 7:00–12:00]**
The Course tells us:
“You see what you expect, and you expect what you invite.” (T-12.VII.5:1)
When we judge someone, we lock them into a role. But we also lock *ourselves* into a role— the one who must protect, correct, control, or be right.
Let’s ask:
**“What role am I playing when I judge this person?”** The fixer? The victim? The teacher? The one who knows better?
Let the identity arise.
And now gently ask: **“Is this who I am? Or is this just another mask?”**
Drop beneath it. Back into presence.
Say:
“I release you from the role I gave you. I release myself from the role I played. I choose to see again.”
Let that be felt.

**✨ [Phase 3 – The Practice of Looking Past Error – 12:00–17:00]**
“Do not see error. Look past it.” (T-9.IV.1:2)
This is not denial. It is vision.
Bring the person back into your awareness. But this time, don’t look at what they did. Look through it.
Let the veil fall away. See the innocence they forgot. The light that remains unchanged.
Say:
“You are not what I made of you. You are still the light. I see the Christ in you.”
This is not just for them. This is how you set yourself free.
Forgiveness doesn’t make the illusion real. It looks gently through it.
And so we forgive not to excuse, but to remember what’s true.

**🌈 [Closing – 17:00–20:00]**
Let’s return now to the simplicity of breath. Of being. Of soft presence.
You may still notice judgment arising today. That’s okay.
Your only task is to look gently, and then look past.
Say:
“I do not need to bind this person—or myself—to a story. I release the world from what I made of it.”
Pause in silence.
Let peace return.
Let love reinterpret what you see.
Say finally:
“I will see with the eyes of Christ. I will remember what is real. What I judge, I bind. And I choose now to free.”
Thank you.
