**🌀 1.** ***“I Am Not the Storm – I Am the Sky”***
This mindset trains you to stop identifying with emotional turbulence and instead rest in the unshakable awareness behind it. It ends the cycle of reacting and offers peace in the midst of any feeling.

*Theme: Emotional weather, mistaken identity, returning to awareness* **Duration**: ~20 minutes **Integrated Ideas**:
- Emotions are not identity
- Watch, don’t resist
- The Holy Instant dissolves illusion
- Returning to peace is always available
**ACIM Core Quotes**:
“You are not a body. You are free. For you are still as God created you.” (W-pI.rVI.3:3–5) “The storm is never real. You are the stillness in which all turbulence appears.” *(paraphrased from T-2.VI.4)* “The ego is nothing more than a part of your belief about yourself.” (T-4.VI.1:6)

**🌬️ [Opening Invocation – Minutes 0:00–2:00]**
Welcome.
Let this be a sacred pause.
Let this be a soft beginning—a moment where we stop trying to manage the world and let ourselves be still.
Today’s practice is simple, but profound:
You are not the storm. You are the sky it passes through.
Emotions will arise. Stories will come. But none of them define you. Let’s begin remembering.
Feel your breath as you inhale… And as you exhale, let go of all effort.

**☁️ [Phase 1 – Feeling the Storm – Minutes 2:00–6:00]**
Let yourself bring to mind a moment recently where something felt overwhelming. A moment where a strong feeling seemed to take over— Anger, sadness, stress, anxiety.
Can you recall that?
Now, instead of fixing it… Let it be here. Let it rise like a passing storm cloud.
Notice how the mind wants to jump in with stories— “This is bad.” “I shouldn’t feel this way.” “What’s wrong with me?”
Pause and watch all of that too.
And now ask:
“Who is noticing this?” “Who is aware of this storm?”
You don’t need to answer. Just feel into the watching.
This is the beginning of true healing: To let the storm pass, without thinking it is you.

**🌤️ [Phase 2 – ACIM Insight + Inner Inquiry – Minutes 6:00–12:00]**
A Course in Miracles tells us:
**“You are not a body. You are free. For you are still as God created you.”**
The body can feel tension. The nervous system may react. Thoughts may swirl like gusts of wind.
But *you*—the awareness of it all—are untouched.
Let’s inquire now:
Say silently:
“I feel anxiety.” Then ask, “Is that what I am? Or is that something happening within me?”
Try with another emotion:
“I feel anger. Is this what I am? Or is this just a passing storm?”
Pause.
Feel yourself soften into that which sees it all, but is not affected.
This is what the Course calls the Holy Instant.
“The holy instant is this one and every one. The one you want it to be, it is.”
Right now can be your freedom.

**☀️ [Phase 3 – Bringing it into Daily Life – Minutes 12:00–17:00]**
Let’s bring this now into ordinary life.
Picture yourself walking through your day— An email comes in that frustrates you. You’re late to a meeting. A friend says something that stings.
Notice how fast the emotion arrives. But instead of identifying with it… Pause.
Say silently:
“I am not the storm. I am the sky.”
Breathe. Feel your feet on the ground. Let the reaction move through you like weather across the sky.
This is what forgiveness is: Not denying the feelings… But choosing not to believe they define what you are.
Even in traffic. Even doing laundry. Even after a mistake.
You are still as God created you.

**🌌 [Closing – Minutes 17:00–20:00]**
Let’s return now to stillness.
Feel your breath.
Notice that awareness is still here. Even if thoughts are still moving. Even if emotions are faintly echoing.
You don’t need to push anything away.
Just remember:
“The ego is nothing more than a part of your belief about yourself.”
You are not your emotions. You are not your reactions. You are not your story.
You are the peace that holds it all.
Let this be your practice today:
When the storm comes… Welcome it. Watch it. But don’t become it.
Say gently:
“I am not the storm. I am the sky.”
Thank you for your presence today.
You are the peace of God.
Amen.
