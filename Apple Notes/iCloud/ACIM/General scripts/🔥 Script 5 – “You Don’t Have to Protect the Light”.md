**🔥 5.** ***“You Don’t Have to Protect the Light”***
The need to defend yourself drains energy and creates fear. Trusting the light within removes the burden of explanation and lets your presence speak for itself.

**Theme**: Defenselessness as strength; releasing the need to guard or justify **Length**: ~20 minutes **ACIM Quotes**:
“In my defenselessness my safety lies.” (W-153.1:1) “You are the light of the world.” (W-61.1:1) “Nothing real can be threatened.” (T-In.2:2)

**🕊️ [Opening – 0:00–2:00]**
Welcome.
Today’s practice is an invitation into soft strength. The kind of strength that doesn’t require armor. The kind of light that doesn’t fear darkness.
Let your breath arrive gently. Feel the body settle. Let go of whatever you’re carrying. You won’t need it here.
Let’s remember together:
“In my defenselessness my safety lies.” (W-153.1:1)

**🧱 [Phase 1 – Feeling the Impulse to Defend – 2:00–7:00]**
Bring to mind a recent moment when you felt the need to explain yourself… To justify a choice. To prove a point. To control how you were seen.
Maybe it was subtle. A tone of voice. A carefully worded message. A hesitation to share your truth.
Let that memory rise now. And observe it without judgment.
Ask gently:
**“What part of me believed it needed to protect itself?”**
Was it the fear of being wrong? Or rejected? Was it the desire to be respected? To stay in control?
Let those layers be revealed.
Say silently:
“This is the part of me that forgets it is the light.” “And that’s okay. I see it now.”
Pause and breathe.

**🔓 [Phase 2 – Undoing the Armor – 7:00–13:00]**
Now imagine that part of you—the part that wanted to defend— is wearing armor. Heavy. Thick. Constantly on edge.
What would it feel like to set it down?
Not to become vulnerable, but to become real.
Feel yourself taking off the armor now.
One piece at a time:
- The need to be right.
- The need to be safe.
- The need to be seen in a certain way.
Say:
“None of these are required. My truth is not fragile. My light is not in danger.”
The Course tells us:
“Nothing real can be threatened.” (T-In.2:2)
And what you are… is real.

**🌞 [Phase 3 – Letting the Light Stand on Its Own – 13:00–17:00]**
You don’t have to protect the light. You are the light.
Let that be felt now. Soft. Expansive. Free.
The Course says:
“You are the light of the world.” (W-61.1:1)
Feel into that—not as a concept, but as a knowing.
What if you didn’t need to defend your choices anymore? Or your voice? Or your identity?
What if your safety was in surrender?
What if your words could be simple… because your spirit was strong?
Say silently:
“I will let my light speak. I will let it be seen without armor.”
Bring to mind someone you often defend yourself around.
Now imagine speaking to them from your undefended heart.
Even if you say nothing at all.
Let love speak instead.

**🌌 [Closing – 17:00–20:00]**
Return to your breath. To the quiet center. To the safety that cannot be shaken.
Say silently:
“I don’t need to control how I’m seen. I don’t need to justify what I know. I don’t need to protect the light.”
Let that awareness ripple outward… into your next conversation… your next post… your next decision.
Today, if the impulse to defend arises— pause.
Say instead:
“In my defenselessness, my safety lies.”
And return to the calm, quiet strength that never needed protection in the first place.
Thank you.
