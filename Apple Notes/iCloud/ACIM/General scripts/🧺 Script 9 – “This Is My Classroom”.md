**🧺 9.** ***“This Is My Classroom”***
Seeing the world as your classroom removes the idea of mistakes and replaces it with purpose. Everything becomes usable for awakening.
	
**Theme**: Every moment as a divinely given opportunity to learn forgiveness **Length**: ~20 minutes **ACIM Quotes**:
“The world is a classroom… each situation, properly perceived, becomes an opportunity to heal.” (M-4.I.1:1) “Trials are but lessons that you failed to learn presented once again.” (T-31.VIII.3:1) “You are not being punished. You are being taught.” (T-6.V.6:1, paraphrased)

**🧘‍♀️ [Opening – 0:00–2:00]**
Welcome.
Let us begin by allowing everything to be exactly as it is.
No rearranging.
No resisting.
No trying to control the moment.
Take a breath… And let it go.
This is your classroom today. And the world is your perfect lesson plan.

**🍽️ [Phase 1 – The Unexpected Lesson – 2:00–7:00]**
Bring to mind a recent event that felt frustrating.
Maybe a delayed email. A flat tone in someone’s voice. Something not going your way. Even a dish you dropped or a door that slammed.
Pause. Feel how quickly the ego labeled it: “Wrong.” “I don’t like this.” “This is in the way.”
Now say silently:
“What if this is not a problem? What if this is my lesson?”
Let that shift your relationship to the moment.
As the Course teaches:
“Trials are but lessons that you failed to learn presented once again.” (T-31.VIII.3:1)
You are not being punished. You are being invited.

**🧠 [Phase 2 – Reframing the Day – 7:00–13:00]**
Let’s zoom out.
Picture your whole day so far. The quiet moments. The people you encountered. The thoughts you’ve had.
Ask:
**“Where have I resisted today’s lesson?”**
It may be a conversation you wanted to avoid. A task you resented. An emotion you wished away.
Now say silently:
“Holy Spirit, help me see this differently.” “Let me not waste this lesson again.”
Feel the energy of resistance soften.
You don’t need to enjoy the situation.
Only to see it differently.
Say:
“This is not in the way. This is the way.”

**🧘‍♂️ [Phase 3 – Daily Friction as Forgiveness – 13:00–17:00]**
Now bring to mind something small that’s annoyed you lately.
The sound of construction. A comment online. A long grocery line.
Let it arise.
And now ask:
**“What if this is my forgiveness moment?”**
The Course says:
“The world is a classroom… each situation, properly perceived, becomes an opportunity to heal.” (M-4.I.1:1)
What if everything today has been tailor-made to bring up what’s ready to be undone?
You don’t have to like it. You only need to be willing to learn.
Say:
“I accept this moment as my teacher. I release my resistance and receive the lesson.”

**🌟 [Closing – 17:00–20:00]**
Return to the breath.
To the open space within.
To the awareness that nothing has gone wrong today.
You’re not failing. You’re not behind. You’re not stuck.
You are in the perfect moment to practice forgiveness.
To undo attack. To release judgment. To return to peace.
Repeat gently:
“This is my classroom. I am here to learn love.”
“Every annoyance is an invitation. Every moment is my miracle.”
And so it is.
Thank you.
