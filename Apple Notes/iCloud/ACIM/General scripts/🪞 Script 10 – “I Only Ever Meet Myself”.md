**🪞 10.** ***“I Only Ever Meet Myself”***
Others are never separate from your mind; what you see in them is what you’ve made real. This vision transforms every relationship into a healing mirror.

**Theme**: Others as mirrors for your healing; seeing the Christ behind appearances **Length**: ~20 minutes **ACIM Quotes**:
“You see what you believe is there, and you believe it there because you want it there.” (T-25.III.1:3) “When you meet anyone, remember it is a holy encounter.” (T-8.III.4:1) “What you project you believe.” (T-21.In.1:1)

**🌫️ [Opening – 0:00–2:00]**
Welcome.
Today’s practice is a radical one: We are not here to change others. We are here to see how every person we meet offers a reflection—an invitation—to come home to ourselves.
Begin by resting into your breath. Let your eyes soften. Let your hands relax.
Say silently:
“Everyone I meet today is a mirror. And what I see is what I’ve chosen to see.”

**👁️ [Phase 1 – Seeing Through Projection – 2:00–7:00]**
Bring to mind someone who triggered something in you recently. Perhaps they seemed careless, rude, overly confident, anxious, or cold.
Pause.
Feel your initial judgment.
And now gently ask:
**“What part of me am I seeing here?”**
Is this a fear you hold inside? A trait you deny in yourself? A belief you still carry about the world?
The Course tells us:
“What you project you believe.” (T-21.In.1:1)
So we’re not seeing reality. We’re seeing belief.
Let yourself feel the truth of that now.
Say:
“I’m not seeing them. I’m seeing my mind projected outward.” “But I’m willing to see with new eyes.”

**🧘‍♀️ [Phase 2 – The Holy Encounter – 7:00–13:00]**
Bring that person’s face into your awareness now. Look again.
See their defensiveness… their confusion… their anger… And now pause.
Ask inwardly:
**“What if they’re showing me the places I’ve not yet forgiven in myself?”**
Feel what that reveals. Not guilt—just clarity.
And now shift.
A Course in Miracles says:
“When you meet anyone, remember it is a holy encounter.” (T-8.III.4:1)
Can you see the light behind their form?
The shared being? The shared wound? The shared holiness?
Say silently:
“You are not the story I made of you. You are the Christ. And so am I.”
Feel the tension melt. There is no ‘other’. Only the one mind, appearing as many.

**🌊 [Phase 3 – The Practice of Reflection – 13:00–17:00]**
Now think of someone you deeply admire. Someone whose presence inspires peace or strength.
What do you see in them?
Kindness? Wisdom? Compassion?
Now ask:
**“Can I accept that this, too, is in me?”**
The world reflects not just fear… but also the light you’ve disowned.
Say:
“What I love in you is also in me. What I judge in you I’m ready to heal.”
“I only ever meet myself.”
Let that line move through you. Soften your sense of separateness.
You’re not interacting with different beings— You’re interacting with different symbols in the classroom of awakening.

**🌌 [Closing – 17:00–20:00]**
Return to the breath.
To the space of neutrality and love.
To the mind that sees all relationships as healing mirrors.
Say silently:
“This day is full of holy encounters. I am ready to learn from each one.”
“I choose to see Christ, or not at all.”
“I only ever meet myself.”
Let that truth open your heart— in traffic, in emails, in family, in strangers.
You are not separate. You are not alone. You are meeting yourself, again and again, until only love is left to see.
Thank you.
