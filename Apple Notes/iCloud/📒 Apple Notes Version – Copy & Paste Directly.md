**Title:** ✅ ACIM Creator Daily Activation Checklist

**🕊️ MORNING – Set the Frequency**
- ⏰ Sit in silence (2–3 minutes)
- 🙏 Affirm: *“I am the light of the world. I am here to extend love.”*
- 👁️ Visualize: sharing light, receiving support, being joyful and calm
- 🎤 Ask: “Holy Spirit, what would You have me share today?”
- ✍️ Read your “Why” or Vision Statement

**🎥 CREATION – Show Up to Serve**
- 🧘‍♂️ Prepare 30 mins before: breath or prayer
- 📝 Review content plan or bullet points
- 🔴 Record / Go Live (even imperfectly)
- 💬 Acknowledge audience, invite support, extend peace
- 📤 Upload or schedule (Free or Membership)

**🔁 AFTER RECORDING – Reflect + Align**
- 📓 Log what you did + what you learned
- 🌟 Ask: “Was I guided? Did I extend love?”
- 📈 Track: Completion + Subs + Comments
- 🤲 Affirm: *“Let this serve. I release the outcome.”*

**🔋 ENERGY MAINTENANCE – Stay Clear**
- 🚫 No scrolling before recording
- 🚫 No distraction/escapes (masturbation, emotional avoidance)
- 🚶‍♂️ Move + speak vision aloud (walk/stretch)
- 💡 Affirm 3x: “This is working. I am led. Miracles are here.”

**🌙 EVENING – Close the Day**
- 🪞Reflect: “What miracle did I see today?”
- 💬 Ask: “Did I follow Spirit’s guidance?”
- 🌌 Pray: *“Thank you for using me. Let tomorrow be Yours.”*
- 💤 Visualize again: You glowing, your community thriving
