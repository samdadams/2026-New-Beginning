The Awakening Stylist

Here are the **four questions** **Gabby Bernstein** asks each morning as part of her *Daily Design Method* to set your day with intention and positive energy:  
	1.	**How do I want to feel today?** — Tune into the emotions you want to carry with you.  
	2.	**Who do I want to be today?** — Define the version of yourself you choose to show up as.  
	3.	**What do I want to receive today?** — Open to what good things you’re willing to welcome.  
	4.	**What do I want to give today?** — Clarify how you want to contribute or offer to others.  

This practice is designed to shape your inner world first, then gently co-create your outer experience.
