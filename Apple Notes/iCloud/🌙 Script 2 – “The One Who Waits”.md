**Theme**: Delays, time-based anxiety, waiting as a Holy Instant **Length**: ~20 minutes **ACIM Quotes**:
“Those who are certain of the outcome can afford to wait, and wait without anxiety.” (M-4.VIII.1:1) “Time can release as well as imprison, depending on whose interpretation of it you use.” (T-15.I.15:1) “Delay is of the ego, because time is its concept.” (T-5.VI.1:2)

## **🔔 [Opening (0:00–2:00)]**
Welcome. Let this be a pause from the mind’s urgency. A space to notice how often we are waiting for something— For a result, a reply, a release… or simply the next thing.
Let the breath draw you into presence. Inhale gently… And exhale slowly. Soften your shoulders. Unclench your timeline. There is nowhere else to be.

## **🌫️ [Phase 1 – Recognizing Waiting (2:00–7:00)]**
Bring to mind something you are waiting for in your life.
It might be a reply to a message… A business result… A relationship shift… A sense of clarity that hasn’t arrived yet.
Feel how the body responds to that waiting.
Is there tightness? Restlessness? A subtle anxiety?
Let the feeling be fully present—without judgment. And now gently ask: **“Who is waiting?”**
Is it the Holy Spirit within you? Or is it the small self—the one who believes something is missing now?
Let’s watch the waiting, rather than becoming it.
Say silently: **“I am not the one who waits. I am the awareness that sees the waiting.”**

## **⏳ [Phase 2 – Waiting as Ego Story (7:00–12:00)]**
A Course in Miracles tells us:
“Delay is of the ego, because time is its concept.” (T-5.VI.1:2)
What if waiting is not a fact—but a story the ego tells?
A story that says: “I’m not complete yet.” “I need something else to be at peace.”
Now bring to mind a simple example from your day: Standing in a queue… Waiting for a file to upload… Waiting for someone to understand you.
Watch how the ego speaks in these moments: “This is a waste of time.” “They should be faster.” “I don’t have time for this.”
Now pause…
And breathe into the present moment. Say silently: **“I give this moment back to the Holy Spirit.”** **“I no longer need this to go faster—or slower.”**
Time, as the Course says, can imprison… or release. Depending on *who* interprets it.

## **🌅 [Phase 3 – The Holy Instant in Waiting (12:00–17:00)]**
Let us now shift from waiting into awareness.
Let this very moment be the destination.
Feel into your breath again. Feel the subtle sensation of being alive.
Say silently: **“This is the Holy Instant. This is where peace lives.”**
Imagine now: You are in line at a store… You are waiting on a reply… You are unsure of what comes next…
What if nothing had gone wrong?
What if this moment was given to you as a space to return?
To soften.
To feel.
To forgive.
What if the only purpose of waiting… …was to remember that you are not the one who waits?
Say inside: **“I wait without anxiety. For I already have everything.”**

## **🕊️ [Closing Integration (17:00–20:00)]**
Let this stillness carry into your day.
Whenever the mind wants to rush forward— Pause. Smile gently. And remember:
“Those who are certain of the outcome can afford to wait, and wait without anxiety.” (M-4.VIII.1:1)
You are not falling behind. You are not missing out. You are right on time for your healing.
Say one last time, slowly:
“I am not the one who waits. I am the peace that waits for nothing.”
Let this peace go with you now.
Thank you.
